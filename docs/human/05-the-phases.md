# The phases

We build PriyaOS in **17 phases**, like building a house: first the foundation, then the walls, then the rooms, then the decorations. We finish and test each phase before starting the next.

There are no fixed dates. Claude builds fast, but each phase is only "done" when it's tested and works.

## How every phase goes

1. **Plan**: read the plan, fix anything unclear.
2. **Build**: Claude writes the code.
3. **Test**: on a pretend computer, then a virtual computer, then (when ready) your real laptop.
4. **Done**: everything ticked, a short demo, and this page's status updated.

## The phases in simple words

| # | Phase | What you get | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | **Foundations** | The base plate: Priya's diary, the plug-in blocks system, settings. Nothing to play with yet. | ⬜ not started |
| 1 | **Priya Sandbox** | The photocopy machine: copy the computer, see what changed, keep or throw away, go back. | ⬜ |
| 2 | **Jobs and safety** | Priya keeps a job list and enforces safety: hidden secrets, permission rules, approvals, spending limits, the stop button. | ⬜ |
| 3 | **Priya AI** | The brain: uses all your AI models, picks the best one, saves tokens, learns. You can ask it questions. | ⬜ |
| 4 | **AI workers** ⭐ | The first full experience: "do this" → AI worker does it on a copy → you review → keep. You can try it on your laptop. | ⬜ |
| 5 | **Desktop** | Super + Space bar, answers in plain words, notifications, top-bar icon, settings by talking. | ⬜ |
| 6 | **Looking after the computer** | Safe updates, finding and fixing problems, health checks. | ⬜ |
| 7 | **Priya Rewind** | Undo anything, even by saying what went wrong. | ⬜ |
| 8 | **Memory and tools** | Project memory, your preferences, finding files by description, the tool phone book. | ⬜ |
| 9 | **Real operating system** ⭐ | Installer USB, nice desktop, all normal OS settings. **Installed on your Zenbook next to Windows.** | ⬜ |
| 10 | **Voice** | Talk to Priya: Super + V or "Hey Priya". English and Hindi. | ⬜ |
| 11 | **Priya Vision** | Priya understands your screen and pictures when you ask. | ⬜ |
| 12 | **AI team work** | Many AI workers on one big job, checking each other, through to pull requests and publishing. | ⬜ |
| 13 | **Automation** | Scheduled jobs, morning summary, overnight tidy-up, teach by showing. | ⬜ |
| 14 | **Extra-strong safety** | Internet limits per job, plain-word safety rules, signed add-ons, Secure Boot. | ⬜ |
| 15 | **Phone and teams** | Approve from your phone, continue on another computer, share with teammates. | ⬜ |
| 16 | **Ecosystem** | Others can build add-ons, an add-on store, custom mini-apps, the desktop restyles itself. | ⬜ |

⭐ = big milestones for you.

## When can you use it?

- **After phase 4**: try it on your current Arch (if its disk format allows) or in a virtual computer.
- **After phase 9**: install it properly on your Zenbook. From then on every new phase lands as an update.

## Why this order?

- **0–2 first**: the foundation and safety. Without them, nothing else is safe.
- **3 before 4**: the AI workers need Priya AI to pick their models.
- **4 is the first "wow" moment**: the whole idea works end to end.
- **5–8**: make it nice to use and powerful.
- **9**: package it as a real OS once the important parts are proven.
- **10–16**: new ways to talk to it, bigger jobs, automation, stronger safety, and growth.
