The Priya bar (0.13)

Super+Space opens a bar at the top of the screen. You type a question, press Enter, and the answer streams in below. Esc stops an answer; a second Esc closes the bar. Every state is shown here in light and dark, at real size. Text is example data.

Look for:

  • Is the bar the right size and in the right place on the desktop?
  • Does each state say clearly what's happening and what to do next?
  • After you ask, the question moves above the answer and the input is free for a follow-up. Does that feel right?
  • When asking fails, the question stays in the input so Enter asks again. Is that what you'd expect?

On the desktop

Top-centre, 720 px wide (ui.bar.width_px, at most 60 % of the screen), 96 px from the top. No dimming behind it: your windows stay visible. Clicking outside closes it; an answer still streaming keeps going and is there when you reopen. Hidden is the normal state: nothing on screen, and the bar is kept ready so it opens in under 100 ms.
